How It All Started

What began as a simple tradition — preparing aachar for our family during the mango season — soon became a beloved taste that friends and neighbours couldn't stop asking for.

Our mother has been making aachar at home for over 20 years. Her recipes were passed down by our grandmother, and every jar carries the same authentic taste and love that has been a part of our family meals for generations.

When family and friends kept requesting jars for themselves, we realized that this homemade taste deserves to reach more people. And so, Purwaiya Aachar was born — founded by Nitish Kumar to share our family's love across Jharkhand, one jar at a time.
